# Methods Summary (`sleep_deprivation_eeg`)

## 1. Participants and Experimental Protocol
Data were obtained from the publicly available OpenNeuro dataset `ds004902` (*Xiang et al., Scientific Data, 2024*). Each participant completed two laboratory sessions in a counterbalanced within-subject crossover design (`SessionOrder`: `NS->SD` or `SD->NS`):
* **Session 1 (`ses-1`, Normal Sleep [NS])**: Recorded after habitual nocturnal sleep.
* **Session 2 (`ses-2`, Sleep Deprivation [SD])**: Recorded following acute total nocturnal sleep deprivation.
* **Metadata Verification**: Ground-truth condition labels were cross-verified against the official dataset documentation and internal EEGLAB `.set` header provenance (`Original file: *_NS.eeg` vs. `*_SD.eeg`), confirming that BIDS `ses-1` strictly denotes NS and `ses-2` strictly denotes SD regardless of chronological visit order.
* **Behavioral Assessments**: Subjective sleepiness was assessed via the Karolinska Sleepiness Scale (`KSS`, 1–9 scale, $N=32$ pairs), state anxiety via the State Anxiety Inventory (`SAI`, 20–80 scale, $N=30$ pairs), and habitual sleep quality via the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (`PSQI`). *(Note: Due to a documented column-duplication error in the upstream OpenNeuro `participants.tsv` for `sub-39` through `sub-69` where `PANAS_SD` duplicated `SAI`, analyses were restricted to verified uncorrupted behavioral measures).*

## 2. Automated EEG Preprocessing & Quality Control (`02a_preprocess_v3_conservative.py`)
Continuous eyes-open resting-state EEG recordings (61 scalp channels, 10-20 system, Brain Products GmbH) were processed using a standardized MNE-Python pipeline:
1. **Binary Stream Integrity & Resampling**: Raw `.set/.fdt` files were loaded with an automated binary recovery fallback (`load_raw_eeglab_robust`) that salvaged truncated sample headers (e.g., `sub-44 ses-2`, recovering $236.9\text{ s}$ at $5000\text{ Hz}$). All recordings ($500\text{ Hz}$ or $5000\text{ Hz}$) were anti-alias filtered and uniformly resampled to **$250\text{ Hz}$**.
2. **Line-Noise & Pre-Filtering**: A $50\text{ Hz}$ notch filter ($\text{width} = 2.0\text{ Hz}$) was applied. A $1.0\text{–}40.0\text{ Hz}$ bandpass-filtered copy was created for bad-channel detection and ICA fitting.
3. **Multi-Evidence Bad Channel Detection & Spherical Spline Interpolation**: Channels were flagged only when supported by multiple abnormality criteria: flatline ($\text{SD} < 0.1\ \mu\text{V}$), extreme robust $Z$-score of variance or peak-to-peak amplitude ($|Z_{\text{robust}}| > 5.0$), low neighborhood correlation ($r < 0.35$), and abnormal power spectral density ($|Z_{\text{PSD}}| > 4.0$), capped at a maximum of $15\%$ of scalp channels ($\le 9$ channels). Bad channels were repaired via spherical spline interpolation (`reset_bads=True`) prior to common average referencing (CAR).
4. **Ocular Artifact Removal via FastICA**: FastICA ($n = 20$ components) was fitted on the $1\text{ Hz}$ high-passed data. Ocular components correlating with frontal pole leads (`Fp1`, `Fp2`, `Fpz`; threshold $Z = 3.0$) were automatically identified via `find_bads_eog` (up to 4 components) and projected out of the $0.5\text{–}40.0\text{ Hz}$ analysis signal (`ica.apply`).
5. **Epoching & Adaptive Artifact Rejection**: Continuous signals were segmented into non-overlapping $2.0\text{ s}$ epochs ($150$ initial epochs per $300\text{ s}$ recording). Epochs exceeding $150\ \mu\text{V}$ peak-to-peak or falling below $0.5\ \mu\text{V}$ (flat) were rejected (with an adaptive $200\ \mu\text{V}$ fallback if clean duration dropped below $60\text{ s}$). All **64 sessions ($32/32$ paired subjects, 100%)** passed QC with a mean clean duration of **$267.3\text{ s}$** (median $282.0\text{ s}$, minimum $114.0\text{ s}$).

## 3. Parameterized Spectral & Time-Domain Feature Extraction (`02c_extract_features.py`)
Power spectral density (PSD) was estimated per epoch and channel using Welch's method ($0.5\text{–}40.0\text{ Hz}$) and averaged across clean epochs:
* **Canonical Bandpowers**: Absolute $\log_{10}$ power and linear/logarithmic relative power across Delta ($0.5\text{–}4\text{ Hz}$), Theta ($4\text{–}8\text{ Hz}$), Alpha ($8\text{–}13\text{ Hz}$), and Beta ($13\text{–}30\text{ Hz}$) globally and within predefined regions of interest (Posterior: `O1, O2, PO3, PO4, POz, Oz`; Frontal: `F1, F2, F3, F4, Fz, Fp1, Fp2, AF3, AF4, Fpz`; Central: `C1, C2, C3, C4, Cz, CPz`).
* **Aperiodic ($1/f$) & Periodic Decomposition**: Following the spectral parameterization framework (*Donoghue et al., 2020*), a robust linear regression in $\log_{10}(f)\text{–}\log_{10}(\text{PSD})$ space was fitted over $1\text{–}40\text{ Hz}$ while excising the $7\text{–}14\text{ Hz}$ narrowband alpha bump:
  $$L(f) = b - \chi \log_{10}(f)$$
  yielding the aperiodic offset $b$ and spectral slope $s = -\chi$. Pure **periodic oscillatory power** (`posterior_alpha_periodic_power`, `frontal_theta_periodic_power`) was quantified as the mean residual power above the aperiodic fit $L(f)$.
* **Alpha Peak & Time-Domain Hjorth Parameters**: Individual Alpha Peak Frequency (IAF), Alpha Center-of-Gravity (CoG), and time-domain **Hjorth Activity, Mobility, and Complexity** were computed across epochs.

## 4. Statistical & Machine Learning Protocols
* **Aim 1 (State Reactivity)**: Paired $t$-tests, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, Cohen's $d_z$, and Linear Mixed-Effects Models (`feature ~ condition + session_order + clean_duration_z + (1|subject)`) with Benjamini–Hochberg False Discovery Rate (FDR, $\alpha = 0.05$) correction.
* **Aim 2 (Trait Stability)**: Two-way random-effects absolute agreement $\text{ICC}(A,1)$ (`ICC2`) and mixed-effects consistency $\text{ICC}(C,1)$ (`ICC3`) alongside Pearson $r$ and Spearman $\rho$.
* **Aim 3 & Aim 5 (Multivariate Coupling & Incremental Validity)**: Partial Least Squares (PLS) with 5,000 permutations and 2,000 Procrustes-aligned bootstraps; hierarchical OLS regression testing incremental variance explained ($\Delta R^2$ $F$-test) by EEG changes beyond baseline state and `PSQI`; and 2,000-sample bootstrap mediation analysis.
* **Aim 4 (Machine Learning Benchmark)**:
  * *Task 4A (Brain-State Decoding)*: Within-subject centered EEG features evaluated under strict **Leave-One-Subject-Out (LOSO)** cross-validation across 5 classifiers (L2 Logistic Regression, Linear SVM, RBF SVM, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting).
  * *Task 4B (Vulnerability Prediction)*: 5-fold outer / 4-fold inner **Nested Cross-Validation** (`RobustScaler` encapsulated inside `Pipeline`) comparing Ridge, ElasticNet, Linear SVR, and Dummy regressor for predicting out-of-fold $\Delta\text{KSS}$.
